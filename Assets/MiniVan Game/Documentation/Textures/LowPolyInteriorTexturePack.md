# Low-Poly Interior Texture Pack

Procedurally generated texture set for apartment interiors, stairwells, and doors.

- Wallpaper_Room: 5 room and hall wallpaper variants
- Tile_Bathroom: 5 bathroom and toilet tile variants
- Wallpaper_Kitchen: 5 kitchen wallpaper variants
- Stairwell: 1 wall and 1 floor finish
- Doors: 5 apartment door surface variants
- Floor_Apartment: 5 apartment floor variants
- Materials: 27 Unity materials generated from the textures

Textures are stored under `Textures/Panelka/Interior/LowPolyPack` and their
materials are stored under `Materials/Panelka/Interior/LowPolyPack`.

Wall and floor textures use Repeat wrapping. Door textures use Clamp wrapping.
The two-color stairwell wall repeats horizontally and clamps vertically.
